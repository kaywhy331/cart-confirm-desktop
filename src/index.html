One setup, reusable defaults

Item defaults

Tax and store order-total allowances

Final-order caps are calculated as item subtotal + configured tax + the retailer allowance. Tax defaults to a conservative 12%; each $30 allowance is an additional shipping/fee cushion. Standard shipping is commonly free above $35 at these stores, but Cart Confirm never relies on that threshold.
