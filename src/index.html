Chrome companion

Port —
  1. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Select Load unpacked and choose the companion folder.
  4. Keep this app open and remain signed in to the stores you plan to use.

The companion sends only milestone names, store/SKU, seller label, observed unit price and final total, a query-free page address, and timestamps to this computer. It never sends cookies, passwords, addresses, payment details, or order numbers. It does not solve CAPTCHAs, bypass queues, or defeat store controls.
